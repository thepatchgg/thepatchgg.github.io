How to get the Amethyst Penguin

Earn Stars from daily logins, spend 28 Stars on a Gemstone Egg, and hatch it. The official update gives the Amethyst Penguin a 0.05% chance.

Current value status

The Patch has not assigned a trade value on release day. The pet is searchable in Trade Calculator, but calculations remain disabled until reliable current market evidence supports a value instead of launch speculation.

What else is in the Gemstone Egg?

The published pool includes Ginger Cat, Toucan, Starfish, Pomeranian, Orca, Golden Griffin, Golden Dragon, Golden Unicorn, Diamond Griffin, and Diamond Dragon. Adopt Me published category odds but not individual rates for those ten pets.
