How to get Cake Friend

The official 9th Birthday Party Notes list Cake Friend as an Ultra-Rare pet available for 99 Robux.

The update also uses Cake Friend's sneezes to send cake slices across the map. Each slice rewards 1 Buck and gives a small speed boost.
