How to redeem codes in Adopt Me

Redeeming a code takes about 10 seconds. Here's exactly how to do it.

  1. Open Adopt Me in Roblox and join a server.
  2. Find the Twitter bird icon on the right side of your screen.
  3. Click it to open the code redemption box.
  4. Type or paste your code exactly as written - codes are case-sensitive.
  5. Press the green checkmark to redeem. Your reward appears instantly.
