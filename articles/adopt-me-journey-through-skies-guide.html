June 12 update is live

Journey Through Skies went live on Friday, June 12, 2026. This is the final Journey to Summer Camp buildup stop, and the main chase is the Rare Pilot Gull for 6,900 Compass Coins.

What changed in Journey Through Skies

The final leg of the Journey to Summer Camp takes the event into the air. Instead of riding Beaver Bob's van or cleaning the river, players join the airship, collect Compass Coins in the sky, and grab flight feathers so Pilot Gull will land and become adoptable.

The official notes confirm one new pet for the June 12 update itself: Pilot Gull, a Rare pet. They also preview a second new pet, Sheepdog Ducky, for Admin Abuse on Saturday, June 13, 2026.

How to get Pilot Gull

Start with the airship activity. The official notes say the sky is filled with Compass Coins, then players take the pilot's seat, collect flight feathers, and adopt Pilot Gull after collecting all the feathers from the activity.

If you are choosing between the pet and the new accessories, buy the pet first unless you already have enough Compass Coins for everything. Pilot Gull is the only June 12 pet with a confirmed price and rarity, so it is the update's safest collection target.

What to know about Sheepdog Ducky

Sheepdog Ducky is not part of the normal June 12 shop table yet. The official post says it debuts during Admin Abuse on June 13 and points players to the in-game countdown.

That means the name and debut date are official, but the rarity, price, exact obtain method, and trade behavior still need follow-up once the Admin Abuse event is live. Treat any early value claim as speculation until the pet is actually obtainable and market offers start forming.

Are Pilot Gull values live?

No Pilot Gull value is live on The Patch yet. The official price and rarity are confirmed, but a day-one Compass Coin cost is not the same as a trade value. The safer approach is to cover the pet now, then wait for reliable public market references before adding calculator or benchmark values.

The same rule applies to Sheepdog Ducky. Until the June 13 Admin Abuse details are visible, it should stay in article coverage only.

Bug fix in the June 12 notes

The official bug-fix note is small but funny: toys held by Bison should now appear in its mouth instead of floating in the wrong position.
