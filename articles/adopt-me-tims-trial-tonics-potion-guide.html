Quick update guide

Tim's Trial Tonics is a temporary potion crafting event. Complete pet needs or Tim's taskboard quests to collect ingredients, then use Tim's cauldron to brew one of 10 potion effects. This is not a pet release, so there are no new pet values or catalog images to add for May 15.

What changed in Tim's Trial Tonics

The May 15 update adds a short potion-crafting loop instead of a new egg or pet shop. The Roblox game listing calls out Tim's Trial Tonics, potion crafting, ingredient drops from needs, and 10 new effects to unlock. Current guide data shows the event running for one week, with temporary ingredients set to disappear on May 22, 2026.

The loop is simple: age and care for pets as usual, check the taskboard for special Tim's Trial Tonics tasks, collect the temporary ingredients, then brew at Tim's cauldron near the hospital bridge area on Adoption Island.

What to craft first

If you are actively aging pets, start with Bonus Aging Potion. It directly supports the normal Adopt Me grind and should matter more than a cosmetic effect while the event is still fresh. If you care more about money, Bonus Bucks Potion is the clean second target because it converts your playtime into extra Bucks.

After those, Future Sight Potion and Busy Body Potion are the most interesting utility crafts because they interact with the needs loop itself. Goofy, Secret Talent, Polymorph, Home, Choosy, and Preferred are still worth testing if you have extra ingredients, but they are not the first place I would spend scarce drops.

FAQ

How do you get Tim's Trial Tonics ingredients?
Complete pet needs and special Tim's Trial Tonics taskboard quests. Taskboard quests are useful when you want a specific ingredient instead of relying only on random need drops.
Where do you craft potions?
Use Tim's cauldron on Adoption Island. Current guides place Tim near the bridge by the hospital area.
Which potion should I craft first?
Bonus Aging Potion is the best first craft if you are raising pets. Bonus Bucks Potion is the best first craft if you mainly want money. Future Sight and Busy Body are the next-best utility options.
Did Adopt Me add any new pets on May 15?
No. Tim's Trial Tonics is a potion event, so the pet catalog and new-pet image pipeline do not need new May 15 entries.
