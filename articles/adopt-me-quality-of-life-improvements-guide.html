Adopt Me Quality of Life Improvements: Pet Paints, building tools, and Trading Hub changes

Published November 20, 2026·Weekly update
i
Official update, November 20

Adopt Me's Quality of Life Improvements! update is live. It focuses on customization, building, profile tools, and smoother navigation rather than a new pet market.

Pet Paints and Natural Color

The Pet Paint menu now has a Natural Color option for restoring individual pet parts to their original colors. The update adds 25 standard Pet Paints and seven Mega Neon Paints. Standard paints cost 500, 1,000, or 5,000 Bucks depending on rarity, while the Mega Neon Paints cost 49 Robux.

Better building controls

Builders get Free Cam, furniture locking, Undo and Redo, copying and pasting furniture, exterior color reset, a configurable House Spawn Point, Teleport Nodes, and a furniture-collision toggle. The Jukebox gains complete music history through the Lightning Dimension, and the Ambience Editor adds five options.

Profile, Dress-Up, and Journal changes

Pets can pose in player profiles using unlocked tricks, stickers can be removed from a page in one action, and the Journal now shows how many copies of each pet you own. Classic non-animated Roblox faces and Marketplace emotes are also supported in Dress-Up.

Trading and potion changes

Trading Hub phone listings can now be searched and posted from anywhere. Adopt Me is testing two Trading Hub experiences, so players may see either the Trading Hub or classic Trading Servers. Age-Up Potions are now XP Potions, and a 2x Bucks Potion is available for 27 Robux.

Value status

This update does not introduce a new pet, so there is no new pet value to add. Treat paint rotations as cosmetics rather than automatic market moves.
