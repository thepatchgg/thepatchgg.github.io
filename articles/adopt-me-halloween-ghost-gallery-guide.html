Ghost Gallery and the Crypt

Use your Ghost Blaster to uncover ghosts hiding in furniture; your pet helps locate them. Beating the boss earns Candy Corn and one Rusty Key. Daily tasks supply four more Rusty Keys. Open Crypt graves for Candy Corn, Twigs, or access deeper underground. Earning Mummy Spider resets the Crypt.

+
Make a simple session plan

Pick one goal before you start playing: saving for a pet, exploring the Crypt, or collecting items. Check what you already own and write down the remaining cost. That makes it easier to stop spending on small extras when you are close to a bigger purchase.

Other purchases to budget for

Ghost Blaster: 7,800 Candy Corn; Grappling Hook: 5,500; Ghoul Mobile: 44,500; Orbweaver Glider: 59,000; Nest Stroller: 11,000. Spirit Goggles cost 4,800 Candy Corn, Jumpscare Badge 600, Black Water Boots 2,100, and Prop Whip 1,950. Jack-o'-lantern and Cat-o'-lantern each cost 40 Bucks; Glimmery Ivory Paint costs 1,000 Bucks.

Before you trade a Halloween pet

A shop price describes how to obtain a pet; it does not establish its player-to-player trade value. Compare actual offers, including potion and Neon status, before accepting. If you want a pet for your own collection, decide what you are comfortable giving up before opening a trade.

This guide does not assign launch values to these pets. Use the trade calculator for supported pets, and avoid substituting an unrelated pet's number for an unpriced release.
