Quick update guide

Stormy Ducky Drama rolled out on Friday, May 22, 2026, around the 11:15 a.m. ET update window. The headline pieces are the Rubber Ducky Box, the premium Shadow Dragon Ducky, and a storm activity that pays Bucks when you find the runaway animals. The next day's Admin Abuse window also added Dirty Ducky for a very short 500 Bucks purchase window.

What changed in Stormy Ducky Drama

A storm cloud has moved over Adoption Island, and the update turns that theme into a short repeating task. When lightning strikes the truck, Duckies run off to play in the mud. Find the three animals, earn Bucks for each one, and collect a completion bonus if you finish the set.

The bigger trading story is the new Ducky pet wave. One pet is a direct Robux buy, four come from a Bucks box with published odds, and Dirty Ducky came through the May 23 Admin Abuse event. The odds matter because the Legendary pull is extremely rare: Strawberry Shortcake Ducky is only 0.5% from the box.

How the storm activity works

The official notes say the storm activity repeats every 20 minutes. Each run asks you to find three animals after lightning hits the truck. You get 10 Bucks per animal, then a 30 Buck completion bonus for finishing the task.

That makes a full clear worth up to 60 Bucks. It will not fund a huge box-opening spree by itself, but it does chip away at the 500 Buck box price while you keep normal pet-care tasks moving in the background.

+
Best first move

If you are playing free-to-play, spend Bucks on Rubber Ducky Boxes only after you are comfortable with the odds. If you want the premium pet, Shadow Dragon Ducky is the direct route, but it is still too early to treat early trade offers as settled value.

Are the new Ducky values live?

Yes. The Patch refreshed the calculator layer on May 26, 2026, after the public tracker feed added the Ducky pets. Rubber Ducky, Glyptodon Ducky, Unicorn Ducky, Strawberry Shortcake Ducky, Shadow Dragon Ducky, and Dirty Ducky now have live calculator lanes and generated pet pages.

The clean read is still cautious: values are no longer blank, but these are fresh-event pets and early trades can swing quickly. Be extra careful with overpays for the 0.5% Legendary, the 99 Robux Ultra-Rare, and the short-window Dirty Ducky.

Bug fixes in the May 22 notes

This update also cleaned up several recent issues. Unnamed pets that receive a Birthday present now show their pet name instead of a blank label. Home Potions now place players correctly at the front door on Premium plots instead of sending them into the void. The team also fixed issues from Admin Abuse week, including the Party Zone Need.

The notes also mention fixes for avatar effect and aura purchases in the Avatar Editor, plus a Santa gift change: buying a gift from Santa now auto-equips it instead of sending it straight to your Backpack.

FAQ

How much does the Rubber Ducky Box cost?
The Rubber Ducky Box costs 500 Bucks and can hatch Rubber Ducky, Glyptodon Ducky, Unicorn Ducky, or Strawberry Shortcake Ducky.
What is the rarest Rubber Ducky Box pet?
Strawberry Shortcake Ducky is the rarest listed pull at 0.5%. Unicorn Ducky is next at 5%, Glyptodon Ducky is 12%, and the Common Rubber Ducky is 82.5%.
Is Shadow Dragon Ducky Legendary?
No. The official notes list Shadow Dragon Ducky as an Ultra-Rare pet sold separately for 99 Robux.
How did players get Dirty Ducky?
Dirty Ducky was a short-window Admin Abuse pet from May 23, 2026. It was sold for 500 Bucks during that event and is now mainly a trade-only pet.
Should I trade for Ducky pets today?
Only if you are comfortable with early-update volatility. The calculator now has tracker-backed lanes, but fresh event pets can still move quickly while supply and demand settle.
Does the storm task pay enough for a box?
A perfect storm task pays up to 60 Bucks, so it helps, but it does not cover a 500 Buck box on its own. Treat it as a repeatable bonus alongside normal pet-care earnings.
