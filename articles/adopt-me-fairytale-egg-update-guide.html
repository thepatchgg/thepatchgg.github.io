The Fairytale Egg is live

Adopt Me published Fairytale Egg! on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The official post confirms a new Gumball Machine egg, all 10 pet names and rarities, and a four-egg bundle for 99 Robux.

Fairytale Egg odds and individual pet chances

The live rarity split is 35% Common, 30% Uncommon, 20% Rare, 12% Ultra-Rare, and 3% Legendary. There are two pets in every rarity, so each pet receives half of its rarity lane.

Price, hatch route, and bundle

A single Fairytale Egg costs 750 Bucks and takes five tasks to hatch. Adopt Me also advertises a bundle of four Fairytale Eggs for 99 Robux. The egg replaced the Endangered Egg in the Gumball Machine.

The individual pet pages also record current secondary hatch routes shown in the live game, including Basic, Crystal, and Admin Abuse Eggs where applicable. Those routes can rotate, so the Fairytale Egg remains the cleanest permanent reference for this release lineup.

Fairytale Egg pet values

All 10 pets now have full normal, Neon, Mega, Fly, and Ride lanes in the trade calculator. The September 5 no-potion estimates run from 0.012 for Pinkypillar and Ms. Muffet to 0.50 for Three Blind Mice; Huntsman Robin is 0.31.

These are dated launch-market estimates from current Elvebredd variant tables reviewed through JaeJaeValues, not guaranteed trade prices. Several pets have already moved sharply since release, so check the date and compare actual offers before accepting.
