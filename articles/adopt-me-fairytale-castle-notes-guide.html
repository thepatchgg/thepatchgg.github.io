Trading Hub, NPC, and bug-fix changes

  • Trading Hub listings now include an offer button so players can suggest items.
  • Human NPCs are now 20% larger to stand out from players.
  • The Personal Stylist task tutorial now opens correctly from the Task Board.
  • Polymorph Potions should no longer occasionally prevent pets from being equipped.

Fairytale Coloring Giveaway

The official Discord coloring giveaway accepts entries using at least three distinct colors through Thursday, September 10, 2026. The announced prize is a Neon Ballet Swan plus Fly and Ride Potions. Entrants must be Level 5 in the official Discord server, and AI-generated entries do not qualify.

What to do next

If you are buying for your collection, the official price is fixed at 600 Robux. If you are trading, use the calculator lanes as a current reference, compare actual offers, and avoid treating a one-day-old estimate as settled.
