Lightning Dimension · November 6 update

Lightning Dimension: choose your bait, then chase four new pets

Published November 8, 2026·The Patch Staff
⚡
Pick the legendary you want before buying bait

The official November 6 notes introduce a permanent Lightning Dimension. Cyclone Sponge Bait rewards Haetyphoon; Storm Chip Cookie Bait rewards Flash Weasel. Both can reward Thunderfisher and Electric Hornet.

How the Lightning Bottle loop works

  1. Enter the new dimension through the portal and collect Forks in a Bottle.
  2. Place them near the storm to collect Lightning in a Bottle.
  3. Exchange three Lightning Bottles in Bore's Cave for either bait.
  4. Put the bait in your pet-pen lure and wait two hours for a reward.

The free infinite bait supply has ended. The portal will remain permanently available after moving to the Sky Castle, so the notes give no limited-time deadline for collecting these pets.

A small chance never becomes a guarantee

Missing a legendary repeatedly does not mean your next reward must be one. Set a collection goal you can enjoy without treating past attempts as a promise of a payout.

Other rewards and fixes

The lure can also give Bucks, potions, stickers, pet wear, and toys. The update fixes Future Sight Potion gold-need text, prevents feeding Polymorph Potions to the temporary 2D Kitty, improves sticker flips, and corrects internal item labels.

Trade value status

These four pets are searchable in the calculator with values pending. Market references currently disagree substantially, so a release-day number would give a misleading sense of certainty. Check several current offers and compare the same potion, Neon, or Mega version. A reward chance and a bait's purchase cost do not establish a trade value.

Eggs and the Halloween transition

This release adds lure rewards rather than an egg hatch pool. The Fairytale Egg remains the latest Gumball Machine egg in the official news coverage. The Halloween Wagon has left; treat the Wagon guide as a record of the earlier transition.
