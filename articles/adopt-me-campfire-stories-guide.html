Campfire Stories is live

The official Campfire Stories Notes post is live for Friday, June 26, 2026. The update adds the Bone in a Bottle beach loop, Setter Treasure Chests, three new pets, new pet wear, a Robux jetpack, stickers, and a short bug-fix list.

What changed in Campfire Stories

The new loop starts on the beach. Once every hour, a Bone in a Bottle washes ashore. Players can hold up to three bottles at a time, then take them to the Irish Setter.

When the Irish Setter receives a bone, it digs up a Setter Treasure Chest. The chest gives a Campfire Story Page plus one extra reward, and those pages are the path to Stygian Owl.

How Bone in a Bottle works

The official notes say one Bone in a Bottle appears on the beach every hour. If you already have three, no more bottles will appear until you trade them in, so make regular hand-ins instead of letting your bottle cap sit full.

Take each bottle to the Irish Setter. The Setter tries to bury the bone, finds a Treasure Chest instead, and gives that chest to you. Every chest includes a Campfire Story Page, which makes the Stygian Owl chase predictable even if the bonus reward varies.

Summer Camp sticker pack

The update adds the Summer Camp 2026 Sticker Pack for 1,000 Acorns. The official odds range from Common stickers at 35% down to the Solaris Animated Sticker at 0.05%.

The named stickers include Irish Setter, Clubtail Dragonfly, Tortuga De La Isla, Ice Cream Hermit Crab, Pine Marten, Red Cardinal, Aestus, Tealwood Monster, Rainbow Trout, Dragonfly, and the animated Solaris chase sticker.

Bug fixes in Campfire Stories

The June 26 notes include four bug fixes: the Skydiving Time Trial should stop adding an extra four seconds, the Skydiving Time UI is smaller on mobile, Field Mouse and Binturong age bars no longer sit too low, and Bio Bushes and Trees can be recolored while furniture multicoloring is disabled.
