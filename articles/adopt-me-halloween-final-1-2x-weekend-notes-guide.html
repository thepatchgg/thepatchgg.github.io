Halloween Wagon: November 2 timing, final-weekend notes, and what leaves

Published November 2, 2026·The Patch Staff
i
The Halloween Wagon has left

The official November 6 notes confirm the Wagon is gone. This guide records the November 2 transition. See the Lightning Dimension guide for the current release.

i
November 2 is the Wagon transition day

The official October 30 notes schedule Bree and Bruno's Halloween Wagon for Monday, November 2, 2026 at the usual update time. Check in-game for the switch; the notes do not give a clock time or a Wagon closing date.

What changed after the final 1.2x weekend?

The October 30 update offered 20% extra Candy Corn during Halloween's last weekend. That was the weekend boost, rather than a new pet release or a promise that the multiplier continues during the Wagon phase. Rusty Keys and other temporary Halloween items were meant to be used before the event left.

How to plan your remaining Candy Corn

Check the Wagon's current stock before buying extras. Choose the pets or items you want first, then compare the total cost with your balance. The official notes describe a last spending opportunity for Halloween pets, vehicles, pet wear, and toys, with the exclusions above. Do not assume temporary activities or Crypt materials remain usable after the transition.

Trade values after Halloween

A purchase cost does not set a pet's trade value. Availability changes can affect offers, but they do not guarantee an increase. These pets now have tracker-backed calculator values; compare real offers and potion, Neon, and Mega status before accepting a trade.
