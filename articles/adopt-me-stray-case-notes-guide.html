Stray Case is live

The official Tux & Shepherd on the Stray Case Notes! post went live on Friday, August 14, 2026. The update adds a daily clue task, two new pets, pet wear, and Trading Hub cleanup.

What changed in the update

The new loop starts with a Mysterious Stranger asking for help. Inspector Shepherd brings in Tux Kitt, and the task is to knock on the stranger's door, collect 6 Bundles of Forks, and turn them in for 75 Bucks. The official notes say you can do it once every in-game day.

The update also introduces two new pets on the Stray Case. Chihuahua hatches from the Basic Egg and Crystal Egg, while Tuxedo Cat is a direct 200 Robux Ultra-Rare.

Best early read

Chihuahua is the pet to watch if you care about the egg lane, while Tuxedo Cat is the clean premium comparison point. Both are too fresh for calculator numbers, so treat early trades as noise until real market evidence shows up.

Trading Hub cleanup

The official notes also include a cleanup pass for the Trading Hub. Canceling or declining an offer is quicker, tablet tooltips behave better, House Edit tooltips show correctly again, and the pop-up text for trade-restricted countries is clearer.

There is also a quality-of-life fix for music behavior during Admin Abuse and 2D Tuesdays, which means the update is not only about new pets. It is a small but useful stability pass for anyone trading while an event is active.

What this means right now

This is safe content to publish immediately because the official update is already live and the pet facts are final. What is not ready yet is the value layer. The Patch is keeping both pets out of benchmark pricing until the market produces enough real offers to work from.

If you want the shortest path from the update into actual trade help, use the guide hub, then move into the calculator or value list after the dust settles. That keeps the site useful without pretending the market is settled on day one.
