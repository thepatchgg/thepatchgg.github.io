Stormy Rings is live

The official Stormy Rings Notes post is live for Friday, July 3, 2026. The update adds the Storm Time Trial, Storm Condor, Oakee Box pets, Burger Bear, Ranger Beaver sculptures, pet wear, toys, furniture, Bee Nice merch rewards, and a small Admin Abuse performance fix.

What changed in Stormy Rings

The new activity is the Storm Time Trial. Players dive through 26 Storm Rings, and the official notes tie the challenge to a chance at the new Ultra Rare Storm Condor.

The pet drop also expands Summer Camp with a new Oakee Box and a premium Burger Bear. Oakee Box has three pet outcomes, while Burger Bear is a direct 99 Robux Ultra Rare pet.

The Oakee Box is listed as a Legendary box for 12,000 Acorns. The common pull is the Uncommon Oakee, the middle pull is the Ultra Rare Oakee Knight, and the chase pull is the Legendary Oakee Wizard.

If you only care about guaranteed pets, Storm Condor and Burger Bear are cleaner targets than Oakee Box pulls. If you are chasing Oakee Wizard, treat the 4% odds as the real cost driver and avoid pricing trades off one lucky box opening.

Fast food pet wear and Bee Nice rewards

Burger Bear arrives with a fast-food pet wear set: Fast Food Hat for 800 Acorns, Burger Flipper for 2,000 Acorns, Ketchup And Mustard Glasses for 1,950 Acorns, and Cheese And Lettuce Collar for 4,900 Acorns.

The notes also call out new Bee Nice merch. Players in the United States who are 13 or older can receive tradeable pet wear rewards from the merchandise path: Buzzing Honeypot Hat, Hive Backpack, and Queen Bee Slippers, all listed as Legendary.

Are Stormy Rings values live?

Stormy Rings pet values are not tracker-backed yet. Storm Condor, Oakee, Oakee Knight, Oakee Wizard, and Burger Bear are in the catalog and calculator as market-forming entries while release-day trades settle.

The July 3 value refresh did promote older Summer Camp pets into live tracker-backed rows. Irish Setter, Stygian Owl, River Otter, Rainbow Trout, Tealwood Monster, and Clubtail Dragonfly now have current calculator lanes, while today's Stormy Rings pets still need reliable public market data.

Bug fixes in Stormy Rings

The official bug-fix note is short: Admin Abuse events should run smoother because pet needs are reset for everyone in the server during Admin Abuse, which reduces server strain during the event window.
