This is the latest official Adopt Me post

The official Trading Hub Notes post went live on Friday, July 31, 2026. As of Saturday, August 1, 2026, it has replaced Bee Pass as the newest official Adopt Me update on the news feed.

What changed in Trading Hub Notes

Adopt Me added a new Trading Hub built around a Trading Phone. The phone lets you summon a hot air balloon and travel to the new hub, where Robo Todd handles trade listings and Robo Rob helps you search offers across servers.

The bigger design shift is that this is meant to reduce endless server hopping, not replace normal trading entirely. You can still trade the usual way, but this new system gives you a more structured way to post and search offers while you stay online.

If you already have the Neon requirement covered, the clean next step is finishing the two-hour Guide unlock so you can test the phone immediately. Players without a Neon pet still need to treat this as a future unlock rather than a day-one feature.

Listing costs, limits, and trade locks

Robo Todd lets you post what you are offering and what you want in return, but the system is not free-form spam. The official notes say each listing costs 100 Bucks, you can only keep two active listings at once, and an extra listing costs 49 Robux.

The other important rule is inventory locking. Any pet you list or offer becomes locked in your inventory until that listing or offer is removed, so you cannot equip it, modify it, or trade it through another route at the same time.

What the value guardrails actually mean

The official notes are careful here: the Trading Hub has imbalance guardrails, but Uplift explicitly says this is not a genuine value system. That matters because players are going to read too much into any blocked listing if the guide is sloppy.

The clean interpretation is simple. The hub is trying to stop trash offers and spam, not publish a new official value chart. If a trade is blocked there, that does not automatically mean the accepted trades are fair by live player standards.

What stayed live from Bee Pass

Bee Pass did not leave with the Trading Hub update. The official July 31 notes say Bee Pass stays unchanged for another week, and any unused flowers and seeds will be refunded when it leaves.

That means Bee Pass is now older news, but it is still live game content. If you are still farming Sunflower Friend or Violet Friend copies, the Trading Hub update does not cut that route off yet.

How do you unlock the Trading Hub?
Play for two hours to unlock the free Trading Phone in the Guide, unlock the Trade License in the Safety Hub, and own your first Neon pet.
How much does a trade listing cost?
The official notes say it costs 100 Bucks per listing, with two active listings by default and one extra slot available for 49 Robux.
Can you manage listings from a normal server?
You can check listings, offers, and trade history from a normal server with the Trading Phone, but you cannot create or modify listings unless you visit the Trading Hub.
Are Trading Hub guardrails official values?
No. The official notes say the guardrails are there to reduce spam and very imbalanced offers, not to create a real value system.
Did Bee Pass end on July 31?
No. The official notes say Bee Pass stays in game unchanged for another week, and unused flowers and seeds will be refunded when it leaves.
Is there a new pet in Trading Hub Notes?
No new pet was announced in the July 31, 2026 Trading Hub Notes post. This is a trading-system update, not a new-pet update.
