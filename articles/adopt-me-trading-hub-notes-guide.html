Trading Hub Notes is live

The official Trading Hub Notes post went live on Friday, July 31, 2026. This is not a pet-release week. The update is about trading infrastructure, new search tools, and how players find and manage offers.

What changed in Trading Hub Notes

Adopt Me added a new Trading Hub that you reach with a free Trading Phone. The official notes say you unlock the phone in the Guide after playing for two hours, unlocking the Trade License in the Safety Hub, and owning your first Neon pet. Once you have it, you can summon a hot air balloon and head into the new hub.

Inside the hub, Robo Todd handles trade listings. You can post what you are offering and what you want in return, then manage those offers as they come in. Robo Rob is the search side of the same system, so you can look for a specific offer and see similar matches if no exact listing exists.

Best first move

Use the Trading Phone to find offers, then move into the value list or calculator before you actually accept anything. The hub helps with discovery, but it is not a replacement for your own trade check.

What to expect if you still have Bee Pass progress

The official notes say Bee Pass stays in the game unchanged for another week. If you still have flowers or seeds, the game will refund any unused balance when the pass leaves.

That means the old Bee Pass guide is still useful for reference, but it is no longer the newest live update on the site or in the game.

Is this a new pet update?
No. Trading Hub Notes is a systems update, not a new pet drop.
What should I use after finding a listing?
Open the trade calculator or value list before you decide whether the offer is worth taking.
Can I manage trades outside the Trading Hub?
Yes, the Trading Phone can show listings, offers, and history in a normal server, but creating and editing listings still requires the hub.
Does Bee Pass disappear right away?
No. The official notes say it stays live unchanged for another week.
