Stitches' Spotlight: Copycat, Boohoo, and Shoebill join Halloween

Published October 16, 2026·The Patch Staff
i
Pick your pet before spending

Four pets arrive in the official October 16 update. Buying all three Candy Corn pets costs 120,000 Candy Corn. Copycat is a separate Robux purchase.

Pet prices and where to find them

PetRarityPurchase
StitchesUltra Rare70,000 Candy Corn, outside the Carnival
BoohooRare32,000 Candy Corn, Hotel luggage
ShoebillUncommon18,000 Candy Corn, Hotel guest quest
CopycatLegendary600 Robux, Manor

How Stitches' Spotlight works

Gather Candy Corn around the tent while avoiding the moving spotlight. Getting caught takes away your collected candy. Switch between the two platform levels and use abilities to escape. Target boards provide temporary cover, but Stitches can destroy them.

+
Leave yourself an escape route

Before chasing another pickup, look for your next safe place. Avoid depending on a single board throughout the round. A clear route is more useful than one extra pickup if the spotlight is about to reach you.

Shoebill's Hotel task

Help the Hotel guests settle down using items from the Crypt. Once the guests are calm, Shoebill becomes available to take home for its listed Candy Corn price.

What Copycat's Polymorph means

Copycat has a random transformation ability similar to the Polymorph Potion. A transformation is an ability effect; it should not be treated as proof that you are buying whichever pet it resembles.

Other purchases to budget for

The Jester Dragon Mask costs 8,100 Candy Corn, Stitches Neck Ruffle costs 5,000, and Creepy Clown Car costs 23,000. Blue Boohoo Mega Neon Paint costs 49 Robux. If a pet is your goal, set aside its full price before buying accessories.

Purchase costs and trade values

Candy Corn and Robux prices tell you how to obtain a pet. They do not set a guaranteed trade value. These four pets are searchable in the calculator with values pending while their launch market develops. Compare several offers and check potion and Neon status before accepting a trade.
