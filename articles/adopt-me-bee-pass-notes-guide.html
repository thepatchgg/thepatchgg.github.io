Bee Pass Notes is live

The official Bee Pass Notes post went live on Friday, July 24, 2026. This is a live update with new pets, a refreshed pass loop, and a new house, so the useful question is which rewards matter first.

What changed in Bee Pass Notes

Queen Bee has headed out of the Coffee Shop for Pollinator Season. The new loop uses flower seeds, bouquet requests, and Bee Pass levels to hand out rewards. Two Rare pets are now in the pass: Sunflower Friend at level 8 and Violet Friend at level 13.

The update also adds a Beehive House for 5,450 Bucks. If you like themed builds, that is the most obvious non-pet pickup from the official notes.

Start with the seed price that fits your budget, then work through Bee Pass levels until you hit the two Rare pets. If you are shopping for the house, compare that 5,450 Bucks cost against the pets you still want to keep your spend clean.

Bee Pass rewards worth watching

The pass reward ladder is straightforward. Level 1 starts with a Nectar Fountain, and the middle levels keep rolling out honey-themed furniture. The reward ladder closes with a Bee-jive Jukebox, Planted Terracotta Pots, and the Queen Bee Throne before the final Rare pet appears.
