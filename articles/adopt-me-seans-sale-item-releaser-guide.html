Quick update guide

Sean is back with the Final Stop Shop, a brand-new Item Releaser that pays out Tickets, a UFO buff loop, Mega Neon Paint rotation, and Glormy Crab as this week's headline Legendary.

What landed in Sean's Sale

This week's update is a clearance-flavored drop. Sean takes over Jane and the Nursery Releaser, the Final Stop Shop returns for one last lap, and the headline pet is Glormy Crab before it shifts into the Admin Abuse Egg on May 1. It is one of the busier weekly updates Adopt Me has shipped in a while.

Why this update matters

Glormy Crab is the headline pet

Glormy Crab joins the Glormy series as the new Legendary, sold directly through Sean's Final Stop Shop for 30,000 Tickets. The official notes confirm Glormy Crab moves into the Admin Abuse Egg on May 1, so this week's window is the only time you can pick it up directly without rolling for it.

The Item Releaser is the real mechanic shift

Sean has abducted Jane and the Nursery's Releaser into his UFO. You can trade in unwanted Toys, Stickers, Pet Wear, Strollers, Vehicles, and Wings to earn Tickets. The official notes are clear that Sean does not accept Food items, so do not bring birthday cakes or popcorn to the UFO. This is the cleanest in-game way to convert old inventory into Tickets you can spend on Glormy Crab and other shop items.

Sean's UFO 2x XP and Bucks loop

Every three hours, Sean's UFO will glitch and grant all pets 2x XP and 2x Bucks for one hour. That makes this week one of the strongest leveling and Bucks-farming weeks in recent memory, especially if you stack it with Birthday Magic gifts from the previous update.

Mega Neon Paints rotation

Mega Neon Paints are now in the Salon on a weekly rotation, with one paint at a time available for a week. That moves Mega Neon Paints from a one-time wave into a recurring lane, which is good news for players who missed an earlier paint they wanted.

Final Stop Shop is a Robux clearance

The Final Stop Shop bundles 13 Robux items at 20% to 45% off as a true last-chance pass. Some of these items, like the Firefly Pet, Celebrity Mansion, and Ultra Car Pack, have appeared in Sean's shop in past rotations. This wave is being framed as the final purchase window before they are removed from the shop.

Rarity color refresh

Adopt Me also nudged the rarity color palette this week, so the Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra-Rare, and Legendary tags read more saturated in-game. It is purely a UI refresh, not a rarity reshuffle, and pet rarities themselves did not change.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get Glormy Crab in Adopt Me?
Glormy Crab is sold in Sean's Final Stop Shop for 30,000 Tickets during the April 24 update window. After May 1, 2026, it moves into the Admin Abuse Egg and becomes a hatch-only pet, so this week is the only time you can buy it directly.
What can I trade into the Item Releaser for Tickets?
The official notes confirm Sean's UFO accepts Toys, Stickers, Pet Wear, Strollers, Vehicles, and Wings. Food items are not accepted. Trade in unwanted inventory to earn Tickets you can spend on Glormy Crab and other shop items.
How does Sean's UFO 2x XP and Bucks buff work?
Every three hours the UFO glitches and grants all pets 2x XP and 2x Bucks for a one-hour window. It is a recurring buff loop, not a one-time event, so plan your bigger leveling sessions around it.
Are the Final Stop Shop Robux items new pets?
Mostly no. The Final Stop Shop is a clearance pass for existing Robux items like the Firefly Pet, Modern Mansion, and Ultra Car Pack. The discount range is 20% to 45% off and these items are slated to be removed from the shop after this wave.
Did rarity colors change anything in trades?
No. The rarity color refresh is a visual update only. Pet rarities themselves did not change, and existing trade lanes still work the same way.
