All four new cafe cats

Cattuccino, Catte, and Matcha Cat come from the Kitty Biscuit. The fourth cat, Pain au Chat, is Ultra-Rare and comes from spending tickets on Basic or Crystal Eggs at the Releaser Stand. The official notes do not publish Pain au Chat's ticket cost or pull rate.

The Throwback Egg can hatch Blue Cat or Pink Dog. Those names are intentional anniversary switcheroos, not Blue Dog or Pink Cat. Adopt Me did not publish their individual odds or rarities in Cat Cafe Notes, so The Patch does not assume a 50/50 split or assign unsupported rarity labels.

Use the official percentages before buying Kitty Biscuits, compare the 400 Bucks Throwback Egg with your other Bucks priorities, and treat early trade offers as a fast-moving launch market. Check the linked Pet Dex pages for each cat's verified origin and current value status.
