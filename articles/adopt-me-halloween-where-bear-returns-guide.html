Where Bear? Returns: Count Slothula and Stray Cat Dragon arrive

Published October 23, 2026·The Patch Staff
i
Two pets, two purchase paths

The official October 23 update adds Count Slothula for Robux and Stray Cat Dragon for Candy Corn. Their trade values are still forming, so purchase price is not a guaranteed trade value.

New pets and confirmed costs

PetRarityHow to get it
Count SlothulaUltra Rare99 Robux, Haunted Manor
Stray Cat DragonLegendary120,000 Candy Corn after using Polymorphing Cat Food from the Crypt

Where Bear? is back

The returning lab minigame places players as Bears, Hunters, or Survivors. Bears try to take down Survivors while avoiding Hunters; Hunters use the Netzooka to protect the group; Survivors collect Candy Corn and can pick up a dropped Netzooka to become a Hunter.

+
Save Candy Corn before side purchases

Stray Cat Dragon costs 120,000 Candy Corn. Keep that full amount aside before spending on the new sticker pack, Slothula Fangs, or other Halloween extras if the Legendary is your priority.

How Stray Cat Dragon works

Feed the stray cat around the map with Polymorphing Cat Food from the Crypt. The official notes confirm that the cat transforms into Stray Cat Dragon; the 120,000 Candy Corn price applies once the pet is revealed.

Count Slothula and Halloween extras

Count Slothula is an Ultra-Rare pet in the Manor for 99 Robux. The update also adds Slothula Fangs for 5,000 Candy Corn and a Halloween 2026 Sticker Pack for 750 Candy Corn.

Trade value status

Both new pets are searchable with a pending value status while the launch market develops. Compare multiple offers, check potion and Neon status, and do not use Candy Corn or Robux cost as a substitute for a market value.
