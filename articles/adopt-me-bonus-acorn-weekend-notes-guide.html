Bonus Acorn Weekend Notes is live

The official Bonus Acorn Weekend Notes post went live on Friday, July 17, 2026. This is a weekend spending window rather than a new-pet drop, so the useful question is simple: what should you buy before the Summer Wagon arrives?

What changed in Bonus Acorn Weekend Notes

The headline change is a 1.2X Acorn weekend. That gives players extra value on the final Summer Camp stretch and makes the next few days a clean spend window for anyone still holding Acorns.

Bree and Bruno arrive with the Summer Wagon on Monday, July 20, 2026 at the usual update time. Until July 24, temporary summer items will automatically convert into Acorns so players can spend them inside the Wagon instead of letting them sit unused.

If you are still holding temporary summer items, do not wait for the Wagon to do the math for you. Convert the items into Acorns, then decide whether you want pets, vehicles, pet wear, or toys before the Summer Camp shop rotates out.

What is still available

The official notes say this is your last chance to grab the pets, vehicles, pet wear, and toys that have been available throughout Summer Camp. The summer furniture stays in the Build Menu, so you can still keep the camp look at home after the weekend spending window closes.

The notes also call out three pets that are not available in the Wagon: Tealwood Monster, Stygian Owl, and Rainbow Trout. If you want any of those, the weekend before the Wagon is the last safe window to act.

What this means for values

No new pets were announced in this update. That means there is no fresh pet value row to add, and no reason to force new calculator lanes. Treat this as a spend-and-convert weekend, not a market reset.

If you are still deciding where to spend, prioritize the pets or items you actually want from Summer Camp, then use the remaining Acorns on the Wagon pieces that would be hardest to replace later.
