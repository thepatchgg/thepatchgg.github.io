How to tackle Hauntlet 2

Bolt replaces Patchy. Branching areas offer different risks and rewards; choose the same route as your friends. Items now benefit the room. Rainbow Wand clears ghosts; the Phone gives false directions; Key opens a safer, richer route; Golden Key fits its matching door. Heart Potion heals missing Red Hearts, Golden Heart Potion adds one without healing, and Ghost Potion protects for one door.

+
Agree on the next door

Before a group splits, pause and decide which route everyone wants. Talk through your remaining health and what you are trying to collect. A short check-in is easier than trying to coordinate after players have already chosen different paths.

Choose a spending goal

For a predictable budget, pick a direct purchase first and set that amount aside. Treat boxes as a separate chance-based purchase: a published percentage is not a guarantee that you will get the pet you want after a certain number of tries. Decide your maximum box budget before opening any.

Extras in this drop

Pro Ghost Blaster costs 29 Robux, but the basic blaster can beat Ghost Gallery. Slime Mega Neon Paint costs 49 Robux. Hotel pet wear, furniture, a 650-Candy-Corn Hotel Bell Throw Toy, and a 750-Candy-Corn Halloween Sticker Pack also arrive.

Trade values are a separate decision

This guide covers obtaining the pets, not a new market valuation. Early offers can vary widely. Check several real offers before trading, and use the value list for established pets rather than guessing a number for a new release.
