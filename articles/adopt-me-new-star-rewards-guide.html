Adopt Me New Star Rewards: Gemstone Egg, Amethyst Penguin, and every confirmed reward

Daily Stars now buy a refreshed reward lineup, including the Gemstone Egg at 28 Stars, the Royal Fairytale Egg at 14 Stars, temporary auras, potions, gifts, and boosts.

The official update is live

Adopt Me published New Star Rewards! on Friday, September 11, 2026. Every price, pet, and probability below follows that official post.

How the new Star Rewards work

Each daily login earns Bucks and one Star. Stars can be spent on the reward you want instead of waiting for one long fixed reward track.

The three temporary 1-Star auras are Confetti, Dance Party, and Sparkler. Adopt Me also says the new build-limit reward is visible in the Home Edit menu.

The official post gives the Royal Fairytale Egg 0% Common, 0% Uncommon, 40% Rare, 50% Ultra-Rare, and 10% Legendary odds. It does not publish the individual pet pool or per-pet chances, so those details remain unlisted.

What happens to old rewards?

When the refresh arrives, Adopt Me says all unclaimed old rewards are claimed automatically. Players also receive their next three rewards as an additional transition bonus. The previous Star Reward pets move into the Gemstone Egg pool except for Diamond Unicorn.

Best next steps

Claim the daily Star, choose whether to spend early or save for an egg, and check the exact published odds before opening. For trading, wait for real Amethyst Penguin listings and completed offers rather than pricing it from rarity alone.
