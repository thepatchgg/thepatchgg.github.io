⚡ Fastest Ways to Age Pets

🏠

Stay in the Nursery

The Nursery area has food, water, and a bath in close proximity. Task refreshes are fastest when you stay in one area.

🍎

Fruit Stand Loop

Park next to a fruit stand and spam Eat tasks. Combined with Drink, these are the two fastest task types to complete.

📱

Second Device

Log in on a phone or tablet simultaneously. Wash and feed tasks can be done back-to-back without waiting for animations.

⚗️

Save Potions for Legendary

Super Age Potions save the most time on the Post-Teen stage of Legendary pets (~120 tasks). Don't waste them on Common pets.

👥

Play with Friends

Some tasks complete faster with another player present. Coordinate with a friend who also needs to age pets.

⏰

Consistent Sessions

Short, frequent sessions beat marathon ones. The game's task refresh timers reset every few minutes so short bursts maximize task density.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tasks does it take to age a Legendary pet to Neon?

Each Legendary pet requires roughly 540 tasks to reach Full Grown. Since Neon requires 4 full-grown pets, you're looking at approximately 2,160 total tasks.

How many pets do you need for a Mega Neon?

A Mega Neon requires 4 full-grown Neon pets and each Neon needs 4 full-grown normal pets — 16 normal pets total. For Legendary that's around 8,640 tasks.

What does the Super Age Potion do?

It instantly completes all remaining tasks in your pet's current aging stage, jumping it to the next stage without doing the tasks manually.

What is the fastest way to age pets in Adopt Me?

Use Super Age Potions for Legendary pets, stay near the Nursery, loop fruit-stand Eat and Drink tasks, and use a second device to double up on sessions.

Do Common pets age faster than Legendary pets?

Yes. Common pets need around 80 tasks to reach Full Grown while Legendary pets need roughly 540 tasks — nearly 7× more.

Does pet age affect trade value?

Yes, aged pets are generally worth more in trades. A Full Grown Neon is significantly more valuable than a Newborn Neon. Check current values on our value list.
